# Predictions (paper §7)

Review-by: 2028-06-30. These bound the paper's empirical commitments.

## P1 — AI-generated regulatory comments

By end of 2027, at least one U.S. federal rulemaking has a detectable, non-trivial fraction of **substantive** comments generated by AI on behalf of commenting parties (not the agency's own comment-processing tools). Individually reasoned arguments identifying specific ambiguities, not form letters.

**Pass:** agency, researchers, or investigative journalism documents that fraction.

## P2 — AI-discovered legal exploit in production

By end of 2027, a firm uses an AI system to identify and act on a regulatory gap, and the firm or an enforcement agency **attributes the gap-finding to an AI system** in an enforcement record, 8-K, or official report.

## P3 — Defensive deployment

By mid-2028, at least two national governments or the EU deploy AI to red-team draft legislation before enactment and produce **public** reports on discovered ambiguities.
